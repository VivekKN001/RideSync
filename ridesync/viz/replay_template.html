What to watch for

  • Yellow lines are the cost. Each one is a driver crossing town to a pickup. The immediate strategy grabs whichever driver is closest right now, even if that is 3 km away, and the rider often cancels on seeing the ETA.
  • Pink diamonds pile up on the batched side. It holds requests for up to 30 s and solves them together, so riders wait a little longer for a match but get a closer driver.
  • Why it pays: a far-away match is a double loss. The rider is likely to cancel, and the driver spent minutes driving toward nobody.
